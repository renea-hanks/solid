How I Got Here

This wasn't a straight path.
It was a clear one.

I've been close enough to the damage caused by bad marketing, bad strategy, and the wrong experts to know the difference between real help and noise. That proximity isn't something I learned in a course. It's something I lived through — and it became the foundation of everything built here.

When AI came into the picture, I didn't see hype. I saw clarity. I dove in at a depth most people don't reach — logging enough time with the tools to develop real fluency, not just familiarity. I became a member of the Google Developer Program and have been trusted with early access to AI builds that are still under NDA.

I realized most small business owners don't need more tools. They need direction. They need someone who can look at what is actually going on and say: this matters, this doesn't, do this next.

So that is what I do.

What I Believe

Small business owners are not behind.
They are overwhelmed — and underserved.

They don't need complicated systems. They need clarity they can actually use. They need someone who speaks plainly, builds carefully, and doesn't disappear once the invoice is paid.

I don't believe in building dependency. I believe in helping you understand what you're doing — so you're not stuck relying on someone else forever. Every client I work with walks away owning their assets, understanding their infrastructure, and knowing how to move forward with or without me.

That is not how most of this industry works. It is how this engagement works.

What I Won't Tolerate

Agencies overpromise, overcomplicate, and underdeliver.
I've seen too much of it to stay quiet.

They hide behind jargon. They lock business owners into systems they don't understand and can't escape. They make people feel small for asking questions that deserve straight answers.

That is not how this should work. You deserve to know what is being done, why it matters, and how it affects your business. Not in a follow-up email three days later. In the conversation, while it's happening.

Every engagement here is built on that standard. No jargon without explanation. No deliverables without context. No decisions made on your behalf without your understanding.

What It's Like to Work With Me

You get clarity.
Not overwhelm. Not a pile of strategies you'll never use.

We look at what is actually happening in your business — no fluff — and we get to the point. When you leave a conversation with me, you will know what to do, what to stop doing, and why it matters. That level of direction is what most people have been missing.

On hard days, I don't disappear. I am steady, direct, and focused. I will tell you the truth even when it isn't convenient — and I will help you move forward with it. You will not be left guessing where things stand.

Outside of Work
