As an AI consulting firm, we build and deploy custom AI agents for clients. All agent development work is supervised, tested, and delivered by Renea Hanks. AI systems deployed by Solid Solutions Today — including Soli — do not make autonomous decisions that affect users without human review. All engagement scopes, pricing, contracts, and strategic recommendations are produced and reviewed by a human.
