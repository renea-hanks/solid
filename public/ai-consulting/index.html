Strategic Alignment

Most businesses arrive at AI the same way.
Most leave disappointed for the same reason.

A demo impresses them. A podcast convinces them. They buy a tool. The tool underdelivers. They conclude AI isn't ready.

That is not an AI problem. That is an alignment problem.

AI curiosity asks: what can this do? AI infrastructure asks: what does my business need this to do, and what breaks if it does it wrong? These are entirely different questions — and almost no one in this space is asking the second one.

Strategic alignment is where this engagement begins. Before a single agent is built, before a single workflow is automated, the business is mapped. Revenue paths. Operational friction. Human touchpoints that must stay human. Risk surface. Only then does the architecture take shape — and only then does the build begin.

The result is not a tool. It is infrastructure. And infrastructure compounds.
