The Build

What actually goes into one of these.

This is not a weekend project. A proper agent build requires a deep understanding of your business, your clients, and your risk surface. Here is what every engagement includes.

Business intake and knowledge base development. Every service, price point, FAQ, objection, and escalation path — captured, structured, and built into a custom knowledge base that belongs to you.

Voice and tone calibration. Your agent speaks the way your business speaks. Not like a corporate FAQ. Not like a support ticket system. Like the best version of you — informed, professional, and direct.

Guardrail architecture. Every agent has a defined scope. What it answers. What it escalates. What it never touches. Those boundaries are not accidents — they are decisions made deliberately, based on your industry's liability and your clients' expectations.

Human-in-the-loop design. The handoff to a human is never an afterthought. It is built into the architecture from the start — triggered by the right signals, executed cleanly, without friction for the client.

Deployment and testing. The agent goes live only after it has been tested against real scenarios — edge cases, unexpected questions, adversarial inputs. It earns its place on your site before it ever sees a real visitor.
