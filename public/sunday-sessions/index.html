Solo Sunday Session

Solo Sunday Session is two hours, one on one, with a fellow small business owner.

What This Is

A Solo Sunday Session is AI training from a small business owner, for a small business owner. Nobody talks above you here.

Most AI training is built for a room full of people who already speak the language — or worse, for nobody in particular. You end up with a generic slideshow, a rushed Q&A, and a vague sense that you should already know more than you do.

This is the opposite of that. It is two hours, one on one, built entirely around your business and where you actually stand with AI right now. If you have never touched an AI tool, that is the starting point. If you have tried a few things and hit a wall, that is the starting point. There is no baseline you are expected to already meet. There is no question too small, too basic, or beneath the conversation.

Renea Hanks runs every session personally — not a trainer reading from a corporate deck, but a solo founder who built her own AI infrastructure from nothing and now helps other small business owners do the same. She has sat where you are sitting. The goal of this session is simple: you leave knowing exactly what to do next, and you are not alone figuring it out.

01

Two hours, built around your business — not a template

Before the session, you will share where you are and what you are trying to solve. The two hours are shaped around that — not a fixed curriculum you sit through regardless of relevance. You leave with a plan specific to your business, your tools, and your next actual step.

02

Four free one-hour follow-up consultations

One session is rarely enough to see something all the way through. That is why every Solo Sunday Session includes four additional one-hour consultations at no extra cost — check in, troubleshoot, or go deeper whenever you need to.

03

A full year to use them

Your four follow-up consultations are redeemable any time within one year of your original session date. Use them right away, spread them out, or save them for when you actually hit a wall. The support stays available as long as you need it.

04

A judgment-free space to ask everything

You will not be made to feel behind, slow, or out of your depth. Every question gets a real, direct answer — because the person answering it built her own business the same way you are building yours.

Who This Is For

If you own the business, this session is built for you.

You do not need technical experience. You need to be the person who wants AI to actually work for your business — and wants a real person to walk through it with you.

You are starting from zero

You have heard about AI, maybe tried ChatGPT once, and have no idea where to actually start for your business. This session gives you a real starting point, not a reading list.

You have tried a few tools and hit a wall

You are using something, but it is not saving you the time it promised, or you are not sure you are using it right. This session sorts out what is working, what isn't, and why.

You want an ongoing resource, not a one-time call

You do not just want an answer today. You want somewhere to return to as new questions come up over the next year. The four follow-up consultations exist for exactly that.

Why It's Structured This Way

A single session rarely covers everything you will need. So this one doesn't try to.

The two-hour training gives you a real foundation and a specific plan. The four free follow-up consultations exist because the real questions usually show up later — after you have tried something, hit a snag, or thought of the next thing you want to build. A full year of built-in support means you are never paying again just to ask the next question.

What This Is Not

Be clear on what you are not paying for.

A lecture

This is a conversation, not a presentation. You talk. You ask. You leave with answers specific to you.

A sales pitch

Nothing in this session is designed to sell you a bigger engagement. If you want something built after, that conversation happens separately, on your terms.

Generic AI 101

You will not sit through material meant for everyone and no one. The session is shaped around your actual business.

A recording

Sessions are live only. What is discussed stays in the room — which is why people bring their real questions.

Refundable

All sales are final. Your seat, and your four follow-up consultations, are yours the moment you book.
