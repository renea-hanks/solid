Brand Strategy

Your brand is the first conversation you have with every client.
Let's make sure it's saying what you want it to say.

The Work

Brand strategy is not a logo.
It's the thinking that decides what the logo has to communicate.

A logo is the output. Brand strategy is what determines what it needs to say — who your client is, what your market expects, what a visitor should feel when they land on your site, and whether that feeling matches who you actually are.

Every color decision, every typographic choice, every word on the page is a signal. Getting those signals right doesn't require hiring anyone — sometimes it just requires an outside, clear-eyed assessment of what's already there.

That's the starting point here: a real conversation about what your brand is currently saying, and what would serve you better.

How This Works

Consultation first. A build only if you want one.

Most brand conversations start with someone assuming they need a full rebrand. Often they don't — they need clarity on what's not working and a plain-language plan to fix it. That's what a brand strategy consultation gives you.

You walk away with direction: what to change, why it matters, and how to prioritize it. What you do with that is up to you. Some people take it and implement it themselves. Some come back later for the full build. Both are a good outcome.

Market Positioning

Who your business is for, who it isn't, and whether your current brand makes that distinction clear.

Visual Identity Direction

A clear assessment of your logo, color system, and typography — what's working, what's dating you, what to fix first.

Brand Voice

The tone and language that should carry across your site, social, and client communications — and where it currently doesn't.

Copywriting Guidance

Direction on what your words should be doing, whether you write them yourself or want them written for you.

A Note on Fit

This is meant to be useful, not exclusive.

If your brand isn't clearly communicating who you serve and why they should trust you, that's worth a conversation regardless of your budget or where you're starting from.

A consultation costs you an hour and gets you a clear, plain-language assessment of your brand. If you decide afterward that you want it built out fully, that option is there. If you'd rather take the direction and run with it yourself, that's a completely fine outcome too.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does brand strategy consulting actually include?

Market positioning, visual identity direction, brand voice, and copywriting guidance. The goal is clarity on what your brand needs to communicate and how — whether you implement it yourself or want it built.

Do I have to hire you to build the brand, or can I just get direction?

You can get direction only. A consultation gives you a clear read on positioning, voice, and visual direction that you can take and run with. If you'd rather have it built, that's available too — but it is never the only option.

Is this right for my business?

If your brand isn't clearly communicating who you serve and why they should trust you, this consultation gives you a plain-language assessment of what to fix and where to start.

What is the difference between a logo and brand strategy?

A logo is one output. Brand strategy is the thinking behind it — who the client is, what the market expects, and what a visitor should feel when they land on the site. The logo is downstream of that thinking, not a substitute for it.
