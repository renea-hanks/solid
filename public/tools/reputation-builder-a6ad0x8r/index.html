Reputation Builder

Traditional review strategies are built for the human eye. This system is built for how people and AI tools now find local businesses. Ask every customer for a review, respond to each one with specifics, make your profile easy to understand, and know what to do if a fake review appears.

How it works

  • Setup. Enter your business details once. They fill into every message.
  • Asset 01. Track review requests for every customer, with the exact message to send and when.
  • Asset 02. Write a specific response to any review, and track which ones still need an answer.
  • Asset 03. Configure your Google Business Profile and website, and write your answers and weekly posts.
  • Asset 04. Spot a fake review, report it, and keep your profile strong while you wait.

Everything you enter is stored only in this browser, on this device, and is not sent to Solid Solutions Today. Use customers' first names only. To move to another device, choose Export backup here and Import backup on the other device.

Before you send anything

Ask every customer, not only the happy ones. Do not offer a discount, gift or anything else in return for a review. Do not pressure anyone to write one on the spot. Google's contribution policy does not allow incentivized reviews or selectively soliciting positive reviews, and the FTC's rule on consumer reviews bans fake reviews. Read the current rules: Google's prohibited and restricted content policy and the FTC's final rule on consumer reviews and testimonials.

The "Why this helps" notes describe how search and AI tools may use this content. Only those companies know exactly how they choose which businesses to recommend, so treat these as good practice, not guarantees. Google changes its menus and features often, so follow Google's current on-screen options. This is not legal advice.
