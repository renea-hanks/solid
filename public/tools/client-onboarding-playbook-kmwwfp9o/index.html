Client Onboarding Playbook

Boundary and Authority Edition. A plug-and-play toolkit that transitions a paying lead into a respectful client, establishing your expert status, your communication standards and your operating rules before the first kickoff call.

How it works

  • Fill in the Setup tab once with your business details and your rules.
  • For each new client, open the four assets in order. Add the client details and the tool fills in every message, guide and script.
  • Copy each one and send it from your own email or tools. Choose New client to clear the client details and keep your setup.
  • Everything is stored only in this browser, on this device, and is not sent to Solid Solutions Today. Use Export backup to move it to another device.
