AI Meeting System

Most meetings end with agreement and produce nothing. This system closes the gap between talking about work and executing it, from the moment a meeting is requested to the moment every action item is owned and tracked.

Your outcome

Meetings that are shorter, decisions that are captured, and action items that are owned. No administrative drag. No follow-up chasing. No rehashing the same conversation next week.

How it works. Add a meeting. Move through four assets: before the invite, before the meeting, after the meeting, and after the summary. Each asset has prompts that fill in from what you already told the system, so you type the topic and the attendees once. Copy each finished prompt into ChatGPT or Claude. Action items go into one master tracker.

Everything you enter is stored only in this browser, on this device, and is not sent to Solid Solutions Today. Use initials or roles instead of sensitive details, and do not paste confidential transcripts into any AI tool unless your company allows it. To move to another device, choose Export backup here and Import backup on the other device.

How to implement this system

Start with Asset 01. Run the Pre-Flight Gatekeeper on every meeting you schedule for two weeks. Then add the Agenda Constructor. Add the Summary Framework in week three. Add the Action Tracker in week four. Implementing all four at once creates resistance. One asset at a time creates a habit.

Choose your meeting

Add each meeting you want to run through the system. Recurring meeting? Start the next one from the last one and your team details carry over.

Step 2

Work through the four assets

Master action item sheet

Every action item, one list

Every meeting's action items feed into this single master list. Paste the table from Asset 04 and it fills in for you.

Rules for this tracker

  1. Every action item gets its own row. Never combine two tasks.
  2. Owner is one person's name. Never a team or "everyone."
  3. Deadline is a specific date. Never "ASAP" or "end of week."
  4. First step is the next physical action. Never a vague description.
  5. Status is updated by the owner, not the meeting organizer.
  6. Review this tracker at the start of every recurring meeting before any new agenda items are discussed.

Coaching note. Open every recurring meeting by reviewing this tracker, not by asking "so where are we on things?" The tracker is the answer to that question. Make it the first agenda item, every time.
