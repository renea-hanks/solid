Is This AI? The Detection Checklist

Check the signals you observe as the conversation happens. The tool adds them up, shows a live likelihood score, and gives you next steps matched to where the score lands.

How to use it

  • Check a signal only when you have actually seen it. Open "See an example" under any signal to compare.
  • The score updates as you check. It is an indicator, not proof. Some AI is built to avoid these patterns, and some people show them.
  • Everything is stored only in this browser, on this device, and is not sent to Solid Solutions Today. Use Export backup to move it to another device.

A product of Solid Solutions Today. Questions: hello@solidsolutionstoday.com
This checklist gives an indication only. It cannot confirm whether you are talking to a person or an AI. Signals are weighted by how strongly each pattern points to automation, based on the author's judgment. In a 2025 University of California San Diego study, Jones and Bergen found that GPT-4.5, prompted to act human, was judged to be the human 73% of the time. People often cannot tell, which is why this tool asks you to look for specific signals.
