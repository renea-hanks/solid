SOPs in a Day Kit

The AI-accelerated process documentation system. Stop carrying your business in your head. Rank your processes, build your AI prompts, and write your SOPs here. Your work saves as you type.

Your outcome

Three fully documented, delegable processes, built in a single day. The knowledge that lives in your head becomes a system someone else can follow.

Work in order. Start with Component 01, then Component 02, then Component 03. Finish one process all the way through before you start the next.

Component 01

The 24-Hour Prioritization Framework

You cannot document everything at once, and you should not try. This framework identifies the three to five processes that are costing you the most right now, so you start with what actually matters.

Step 01 · Identify your bottleneck processes

Write down every recurring task in your business that currently lives only in your head. Do not filter. Do not judge. Just list them. Include anything you have trained someone on verbally but never written down.

Categories to prompt your thinking
  • Client intake. How new clients are onboarded, what information you collect, what happens first.
  • Delivery. How your core service or product gets produced and delivered.
  • Communication. How you respond to inquiries, follow up, and handle problems.
  • Admin and finance. Invoicing, payment tracking, expense logging, reporting.
  • Marketing and content. How posts get created, scheduled, and published.
  • Vendor and tools. How you manage subscriptions, credentials, and vendor relationships.

Step 02 · Score each process on three dimensions

For each process, score 1 to 3 on each dimension. The total appears automatically. Your highest scores are your Day One priorities.

Reading your scores. Processes scoring 7 to 9 are your immediate priorities. Processes scoring 4 to 6 are your second wave. Do not attempt to document anything scoring below 4 until everything above it is done.

Step 03 · Confirm your top three

Use the Top three box on a process to select it. These are the only processes you are documenting today. Write them down before you open anything else in this kit.

For each of your three, answer this: If I had to leave this business for two weeks starting tomorrow, what would break first because it is not written down? If the answer matches your top three, you are in the right place. If it does not, adjust your list.

Scope discipline. The most common mistake is trying to document fifteen things and finishing none of them. Three processes, fully documented, is a usable system. Fifteen half-finished drafts are not. Finish three. Then come back for three more.

Component 02

The AI Prompt Matrix

Six prompts, each designed to pull a different type of process out of your head and turn it into structured, delegable documentation. Use the one that matches how your knowledge currently exists. Fill in the boxes, copy the finished prompt, and paste it into your AI tool.

Component 03

The SOP Templates

Three templates for three types of processes. Paste in the content from your AI-generated draft, and you have a finished, delegable document. Template A is for linear processes. Template B is for decision-heavy processes. Template C is for quality verification.

Keeping SOPs current

A SOP six months out of date is worse than no SOP. It trains people to do the wrong thing with confidence. Review every document quarterly. Read it, run it against what you currently do, and update anything that has changed. Date every revision. The date at the bottom tells you whether to trust the document. Each SOP above shows a review date 90 days after its last update.
