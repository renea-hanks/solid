Weekly Prompt Vault

75 ready-to-use prompt frameworks organized by the work you actually do every week. Stop staring at blank screens. Start producing.

Your outcome

A repeatable weekly system that makes AI sound like your business, not a corporate press release. Save 3 to 5 hours a week on the tasks you should have handed off already.

How to use it. Choose a day. Open a prompt. Fill in the boxes with your specifics. The more specific your input, the more specific and useful the output. Copy the finished prompt into ChatGPT or Claude. Each prompt shows where it performs best: Claude for nuanced reasoning, tone calibration and structured analysis, ChatGPT for formatting, data organization and structured outputs.

Boxes with the same label share one answer across the vault, so you type your business type once. Everything saves in this browser, on this device, and is not sent to Solid Solutions Today. To move to another device, choose Export backup here and Import backup on the other device.
